### Short bio (home page "Behind the lens", 2 lines)
> I'm Ayush, a wildlife and bird photographer from Nadiad, India, with a soft spot for owls. It started with my mother's phone and a childhood of NatGeo; today it's birding trips and a lot of waiting for the right light.

### Bio (one paragraph per block)

> I grew up in Nadiad, Gujarat, with NatGeo and Discovery always on the TV. Long before I owned a camera, I already wanted to look at animals closely.

> My first camera was my mother's phone. Around 8th grade I started borrowing it, switching to pro mode and teaching myself what the photography terms meant and how the tricks worked, one shot at a time.

> In college I got my first real exposure to professional photography, and I was lucky to have two mentors, Prayag Bhai and Dev Bhai, guide me through it. With their help I moved to DSLR and mirrorless cameras, prime and wide lenses, and learned the things that matter most: framing, reading light, and editing with colour and light that stay true to the scene.

> Birding is where it all comes together. I watch birds from home almost every day and go out birding whenever I can. Owls are my favourite subject, without any doubt: they make you wait, and then give you everything in a single look. Birding is where I practise the most, and where I learned that wildlife photography is mostly patience, then focus.

> Away from wildlife I've walked Ahmedabad's heritage lanes and photographed the stepwell at Adalaj, shot the night sky and concerts, and worked on films, once as director of photography. Different subjects, same habit: wait, watch, and be ready when the moment comes.
